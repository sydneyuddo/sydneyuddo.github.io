About

I’m a senior at Tulane University in New Orleans, studying Political Science and Psychology, with the goal of attending law school. I’m interested in how legal decisions get made and how people experience the justice system.

As an intern in the chambers of Judge Jay C. Zainey at the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, I reviewed case materials and pre-sentence reports, observed oral arguments and sentencing, and took part in chambers conferences. Before college I interned at AWM Law Offices in New Jersey, drafting correspondence and keeping case files organized.

Outside the classroom I co-captain the Tulane Cheerleading team, serve in Phi Alpha Delta’s pre-law congress, and work the front desk at Drybar. Those roles have taught me to stay organized, keep calm under pressure, and look out for the people around me.
